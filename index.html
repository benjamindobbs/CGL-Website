OUR STORY

From humble beginnings

It started almost by accident — a schedule mixup, a Digital Art class with no software and no budget, and some screenprinting gear pieced together from friends and CCSU dumpsters. In March 2022 we made our first run of student-made hoodies.

Since then, students have produced hundreds of designs and thousands of custom garments for Classical, Hartford Public Schools, and local non-profits.
